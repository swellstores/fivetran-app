# Fivetran

Send your Swell store data to your data warehouse through Fivetran.

- **Live sync:** orders, customers, products, subscriptions, payments, refunds, invoices, carts, coupons, promotions and categories are sent to a Fivetran Webhooks connector as they change.
- **Backfills:** send existing records per collection, or all collections in one click, with progress you can follow, cancel and resume.
- **Secure delivery:** optional HMAC SHA-256 request signing that Fivetran verifies.
- **Simple setup:** paste the Webhooks connector URL, choose your collections and send a test event.
- **Event log format:** each change is one row with the full record, so you can build history or current-state tables in your warehouse.
